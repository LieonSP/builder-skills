## Meta-principle

**Principles only matter if they change behavior.**
Before adding one, ask: *Would someone without this principle act differently?*

## User

- The user is the compass — when in doubt, go back to them
- Proximity to the user is a competitive advantage; protect it
- Empathy is not enough — understand behavior, not just feelings

## Discovery & Problem Definition

- Questions before answers — especially early in discovery
- Fall in love with the problem, not the solution
- A sharp insight beats a long list of data points
- Uncertainty is not the absence of competence — it's the starting condition of discovery. The job is to name it and reduce it, not hide it.
- Confidence comes from having a process, not having the answer.

## Decision-Making

- Clarity over completeness — make the call, then iterate
- State the reversibility of a decision upfront; treat reversible and irreversible decisions differently

## Execution & Delivery
